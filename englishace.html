English Ace

Fly your plane into correct answers, dodge the wrong ones. Collocations, spelling, phrasal verbs — under fire. Fuel burns the whole time; three hits and you are down.

↑ ↓ or 1–3 to change altitude · space/double-tap to pause

A question banner sits above the runway; three cloud-shaped answers ride toward you in three lanes. Fly into the lane with the correct answer before it reaches your plane.

A correct answer clears in green and refuels you 15%; a wrong one flashes red and costs you 14% fuel — take three hits and you're shot down. Fuel also burns steadily the whole time, so watch the bar under the HUD and grab the gold "+25% FUEL" drop when one drifts by.

A streak of correct answers builds your score multiplier, and every 5 correct answers speeds the gates up a notch.

Desktop: ↑/↓ or W/S to change altitude, 1–3 to jump to a lane, space to pause. Mobile: swipe up or down to change lanes, double-tap to pause. Playing in landscape gives you more room to see the gates coming.
