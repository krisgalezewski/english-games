A word falls out of its own sentence. Catch it in the right part of speech and it clears. Miss and it stays on the floor. Ten sentences to a level — then the lanes shuffle and the drop gets quicker. Prepositions join at level 6, pronouns at level 11.

Arrow keys or 1–4 to pick a lane · ↓ to drop · space to pause

A sentence appears with one word lifted out and pinned in place. After a beat it drops down the middle of the field below.

Steer it into the lane for its part of speech before it lands — the lanes are labelled along the bottom and shuffle every level. Catch it right and it clears; miss and it stacks up in that lane. Eight tiles stacked in one lane and the run is over.

A streak of correct catches builds your multiplier and, every few in a row, lifts a tile off whichever lane is tallest. Prepositions join the lanes at level 6, pronouns at level 11.

Desktop: ← → or A/D to move, ↓ or S to hard-drop, 1–6 to jump straight to a lane, space to pause. Mobile: tap a lane button along the bottom, or swipe left/right to move and swipe down to hard-drop.
