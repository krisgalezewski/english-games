Word Snake

Eat letters in order to build a word, then submit it. Bright tiles still lead somewhere; dim ones don't, and a red ⌫ tile takes back your last letter. Every tile burns down on its own fuse, so longer words pay more but cost speed.

Arrows, WASD or swipe · Enter/tap to submit · space/double-tap to pause · a ⌫ tile undoes a letter

Letters are scattered across the board. Steer your snake to collect them in order to spell a word — the letters join your current word in the tray as you eat them.

A tile that could still extend your word glows cream and bright; a dim tile leads nowhere useful right now. When your tray spells a real word it turns gold and pulses — submit it to bank the points and reset your tray, or keep pushing for something longer.

Ran into a dead end? Look for the red ⌫ tile — eating it removes the last letter from your tray so you can try a different branch.

The board wraps at every edge — fly off one side and you reappear on the other. Only crossing your own body ends the run. Every 5 words you bank, the snake speeds up one notch.

Desktop: arrow keys or WASD to steer, Enter to submit a ready word, Space to pause. Mobile: swipe to steer (you can change direction mid-swipe without lifting your finger), tap once to submit a ready word, double-tap to pause, or use the on-screen D-pad.
